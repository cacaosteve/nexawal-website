NOT YOUR TYPICAL FINE PRINT.

Trust less.
Understand more.

Self-custody is a responsibility.
We think the software should be honest about that.

01

The keys stay with you.

Your recovery seed and wallet keys are handled on your device, not uploaded to a NexaWal account. Keep a secure offline backup. We cannot recover a lost seed.

02

Open code. Not a security guarantee.

The app source and shared WalletCore are public. NexaWal has not undergone an independent security audit. Start with small amounts and understand the risks.

03

Private money still has a network.

Remote nodes can see your IP and sync/broadcast metadata. Optional fiat estimates contact our rates service over clearnet HTTPS—even when the wallet node uses I2P.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING.

Good questions.

Project discussions & issues ↗
Is NexaWal custodial?

No. NexaWal is a self-custodial wallet interface. You are responsible for the recovery seed and funds. There is no account recovery service or password reset that restores a lost seed.

Is it ready for large amounts?

NexaWal is in active development and has not been independently security-audited. Do not interpret this website as a stable-release or safety guarantee. Review the source and release notes, keep an offline seed backup, and test with small amounts.

Does it support other coins or swaps?

NexaWal focuses on Monero. This site does not offer swaps, trading, a token, or an investment product. The core experience is balance, sync, send, receive, and transaction history.

Do I need my own node?

No. The default public node is https://rpc.nexawal.com. You can configure a different node. A node operator can observe network and wallet-query metadata, so a node you control is a better choice when that matters to you.

How do optional fiat estimates work?

They are off by default. When enabled, the app requests a rate for your selected currency from rates.nexawal.com over clearnet HTTPS, even when your wallet node uses I2P. Wallet balances, addresses, seeds, and transaction history are not sent for rates. XMR is what you hold and send.

Where can I get help or report a bug?

Use the relevant iOS, Android, or desktop repository linked above. Never post a recovery seed, private key, wallet cache, or other private wallet data in an issue. For a sensitive security concern, consult the repository’s security information before posting details publicly.
